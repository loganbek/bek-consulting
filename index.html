About

Bek Consulting is the independent practice of Logan Taylor Bek, a security-focused software engineer and consultant based in Oakland, California, with 20+ years of programming experience.

His background spans offensive security and defensive analysis, Web3 development — he was a founding developer of the KogeCoin DeFi ecosystem on Polygon and has operated a bug bounty program on Immunefi as a program owner — and enterprise software consulting, including Oracle CPQ engagements and a DevOps stint at Facebook.

Current research focuses on manual code review and proof-of-concept development against in-scope DeFi protocols, performed on local forks under each program's published rules of engagement. He holds a B.S. in Computer Information Science from the University of Oregon (2014).
